# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are building financial software for everyone. We pledge to make participation in the LedgerForge community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Expected:** welcoming and inclusive language; respecting differing viewpoints; graceful acceptance of constructive criticism; empathy in code review (the author is not the code); focusing on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable:** harassment of any kind; offensive comments; publishing others' private information without consent; sustained disruption of discussions; sexualized language or attention; any conduct a reasonable professional would consider inappropriate in a workplace.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces: the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, chat, and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at **imsrenpsycho@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed and investigated confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Attribution

This code of conduct adapts the widely used Contributor Covenant pattern, simplified for this project.
